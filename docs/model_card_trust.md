# Model card: seller trust model `trust_v1`

> **All data behind this document is synthetic and the system is not validated on real data.**
> Simulated money, sandbox prototype, not a product of upay or any other company.
> Generated by `scripts/build_docs.py` from `reports/*.json`; do not edit by hand.

## Purpose and intended use

Gives a buyer a **score (0-100), a band and two to four plain-language reasons** (Bangla and
English) about a seller *before* the buyer pays through a Safe Order. The band is one of TRUSTED,
CAUTION, HIGH_RISK, or LIMITED_HISTORY (a neutral band for sellers with little history, so that
new does not mean scammer).

**Out of scope:** any decision about a real person or real money; deciding disputes (a human
analyst decides every dispute); use on real marketplace data without a new, real validation.

## Model

- Algorithm: LightGBM gradient-boosted trees, probability calibrated with platt scaling.
- 13 seller features: `account_age_days`, `orders_7d`, `orders_30d`, `unique_buyers_24h`, `unique_buyers_30d`, `buyer_burst_ratio`, `repeat_buyer_ratio`, `buyer_concentration`, `refund_rate`, `dispute_rate`, `median_cashout_latency_min`, `ticket_vs_category_ratio`, `shared_buyer_overlap`.
- Monotone constraints: more refunds or disputes can never make a seller look safer
  (`refund_rate`, `dispute_rate`); see "Limitations".
- Hyperparameters: `colsample_bytree=0.8`, `early_stopping_rounds=30`, `learning_rate=0.05`, `min_child_samples=20`, `n_estimators=400`, `num_leaves=15`, `reg_lambda=1.0`, `subsample=0.8`, `subsample_freq=1`; best iteration
  85 (early stopping); seed 42; trained 2026-10-01.
- Libraries: lightgbm 4.7.0, numpy 2.4.6, pandas 3.0.6, scikit-learn 1.9.1.
- Reasons come from the model's own per-feature contributions, mapped to fixed bilingual templates
  (`backend/app/i18n/reasons_*.json`). No generative model is involved.

## What the model relies on

Exact tree SHAP values (LightGBM `pred_contrib`) on generator v2 are in
`reports/figures/trust_feature_importance.png` (average size per feature) and
`reports/figures/trust_one_seller.png` (one seller). The model leans mostly on cash-out speed and on
how far order prices are above the category norm, which are exactly the behaviours the synthetic
generator gives the fake sellers: a reminder that it has learned our assumptions.

## Fairness check (honest new sellers)

The blueprint hides every account younger than 14 days behind LIMITED_HISTORY. In the generated
data every `fake_burst` seller is that young: under the literal rule their recall is
0% and overall recall of
high-risk sellers falls to 54.3%. The configured policy
(`override_default`) still shows a limited-history seller as HIGH_RISK when its model score is
20 or lower.

| Policy for sellers with little history | Recall of high-risk | Precision of HIGH_RISK band | Honest new sellers wrongly HIGH_RISK | Honest new sellers in LIMITED_HISTORY |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `blueprint_literal` | 54.3% | 97.7% | 1.0% | 38.8% |
| `no_limited_history_band` | 97.8% | 98.7% | 1.0% | 0.0% |
| `override_default` | 97.7% | 98.7% | 1.0% | 38.8% |

Honest established sellers wrongly flagged on v2: 0.0%;
honest new sellers: 1.0%.
**The override threshold is a policy choice that needs a human decision; it is not a finding.**

## Limitations

- **Synthetic data, not validated on real data.** The behaviour types and their parameters are
  assumptions. The model learned the generator, and the high scores above say nothing about real
  sellers. Real fraud adapts; this data does not.
- Calibration (Platt scaling on 600 rows) is checked in the table
  above; bins with few sellers give noisy observed rates (figure: `reports/figures/calibration.png`).
- A single refund barely moves the score of a long-established seller (it moves visibly only for
  sellers with a short record), which is correct for the model but surprising in a demo.
- The monotone constraints were added after a behavioural check showed that one extra refund raised
  the score of a quarter of the sellers on v2; the measured numbers before and after are in
  `DECISIONS.md`.
- No fairness analysis by region, gender, or any real demographic is possible: the data has none.
