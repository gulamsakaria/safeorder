# Pitch outline (maps to the judging weights)

> **DRAFT for the team to review. Not legal advice, not an upay policy, not a product.**
> Synthetic data, simulated money, not validated on real data.
> Generated by `scripts/build_pitch.py`; edit the script (or copy the text out) rather than this file.

Numbers below are read from `reports/*.json`. If a number says "not measured" do not say it aloud.

1. **Problem and field evidence (relevance, 20%).** Advance payment to an unverifiable Facebook page,
   no way back, and slow argument-based disputes. Field evidence: *not measured* until the team's
   3 to 5 interviews are done (use 3 to 5 anonymous quotes, with consent; do not invent any).
2. **The flow in one picture (innovation, product).** Check the seller, hold the money, report, analyse,
   a human decides, the seller's record learns.
3. **Live demo (prototype quality, 15%).** The seven scenarios in `demo_script.md`.
4. **AI depth (20%) and responsible AI (5%).** Three jobs, three methods, rules kept apart:
   - Seller risk: gradient-boosted trees, calibrated, with per-reason explanations. On a *different*
     synthetic generator it catches 88% of high-risk sellers at 5% false alarms, against 35% for ranking by account age and 39% for the simple "younger than 14 days" rule.
   - Dispute text: TF-IDF and logistic regression over Bangla, Banglish and regional text: macro-F1
     0.78 (test 1) and 0.70 (test 2), on cases written by one author.
   - Rules and flags: contradictions (code used but "not received"), repeat claimants, injection.
   - No generative model decides anything; a person confirms every dispute.
5. **Honest evaluation (credibility).** Baseline against model; wrong-refund 5.3% / 8.5% and
   wrong-rejection 3.8% / 11.1% (test 1 / test 2); fast lane 17% of cases, 96%
   correct, 0 wrong refunds; injection screen 22 of 22 on the phrases it was built on but only
   1 of 12 on phrases written afterwards. **Say that the dispute cases were written by an AI
   assistant, one author for every split, and that real validation needs real or team-written cases.**
6. **Impact (20%).** *not measured.* Loss prevented and minutes saved per dispute were not measured
   (no real data, no timed analyst sessions: `docs/time_study_template.json`). What can be said honestly:
   what the prototype measures (recall at a fixed false-alarm rate, wrong-refund rate, fast-lane share)
   and how a pilot would measure the rest.
7. **Path to production (scalability, 10%).** API-first, ledger and state machine shaped for order and
   wallet events; validation on governed, anonymised data; monitoring (score drift, outcomes against
   recommendations); human-review service levels; appeals; proper seller verification. State what is
   needed from upay: governed data for a validation stage and an integration contact.
8. **Limits, said out loud.** Synthetic data; text only (no image or video analysis); one-author
   dispute cases; Bangla needs native review; held funds may need regulatory approval, so it is shown
   as a future upay-governed feature, simulated here.
