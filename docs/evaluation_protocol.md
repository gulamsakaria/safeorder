# Evaluation protocol

> **All data behind this document is synthetic and the system is not validated on real data.**
> Simulated money, sandbox prototype, not a product of upay or any other company.
> Generated by `scripts/build_docs.py` from `reports/*.json`; do not edit by hand.

## Seller trust model

1. **Training data:** synthetic generator v1 only. **Splits** (stratified by label, seed
   42): 60% train, 20% validation
   (early stopping only), 20% calibration (Platt scaling only).
2. **Test data:** generator v2: a different seed, parameters shifted by about +/-20% and a
   different archetype mix. No v2 row was used to fit, stop or calibrate the model.
3. **What did look at v2, in full disclosure:** the limited-history override threshold
   (20) was fixed before looking at v2
   results, but the three-policy comparison that documents the blueprint conflict is measured on v2; and the monotone
   constraints on `refund_rate` and `dispute_rate` were added after a behavioural check on v2 sellers.
   Accuracy was compared before and after and did not drop (`DECISIONS.md`), but strictly v2 has
   influenced the way the design was decided, so it is a held-out *generator*, not a pristine blind test.
4. **Labels:** headline = `is_high_risk` (about 4% random noise); the clean archetype label is a
   diagnostic only.
5. **Metrics:** PR-AUC and ROC-AUC (ranking); recall at a fixed false-alarm rate of
   5% (the number a product owner cares about, because
   the classes are imbalanced and accuracy would mislead); expected calibration error with
   10 bins; per-cohort false-positive rates for
   honest established, honest new and chronic-poor-service sellers.
6. **Baselines:** the account-age score (ranking by newness) and the simple age rule
   (< 14 days = high risk).
7. **Speed:** 300 timed predictions against a
   50 ms budget.
8. **Leakage checks:** the ground-truth archetype and `ring_id` are not model inputs (the feature
   list is fixed in `backend/app/trust/features.py`); features are computed only from orders before
   the scoring moment (tested).

## Dispute classifier and routing

not measured: no cases yet (Step 5 and 6). When they exist the protocol is: split **by batch**
so near-duplicates cannot cross the train/test boundary; a separate injection set; no tuning on the
final test split; report macro-F1, per-class precision and recall, confusion matrices, the
wrong-refund and wrong-rejection rates and the routing coverage.

## Injection checks

- Phrases the screen was built on: 22 of 22
  detected. This is a **regression check only**: the patterns were written while looking at them.
- Phrases written after the screen and never used to change it:
  1 of 12 detected. Developer-written
  and small, an indication only.
- False alarms on ordinary complaints: 0 of 9.
- Invariance: 176 pairs (injected vs plain evidence, stand-in
  classifier): the classifier text, probabilities, recommendation and other flags never changed and
  human review was always forced. This holds by construction with a stand-in and says nothing about
  a trained classifier.
- The team's held-out injection set: not measured (not available yet).
